A1. ONE PRODUCER, THREE TIME/AUTHORITY BINDINGS
One logical formula producer serves offline replay and online decisions. Provider
adapters normalize evidence; they do not maintain a second set of feature formulas.
Historical inputs are from causal, versioned LOCAL history. Current dynamic inputs
use one qualified Steam GLG scoreboard frame/continuity prefix, with no OpenDota
HTTP dependency or silent alternate live source. Other profiles require a separate
equivalence/availability decision. Legacy serving packages remain version-isolated.

FrozenHistoricalBinding identifies the actual private view: source/corpus reference,
snapshot/envelope, effective facts hash, target derivation, exact series receipt,
immutable cutoff t0, validFrom/identity t1 and native-reference proof where used.
ActiveTargetBinding identifies the host's selected entity/transition, identity
revision and approved frozen historical view for THIS decision.
CurrentSourceFence identifies latest admitted/classified packet, progression,
terminal and STOP epochs, evaluated at owned current time after IO for LIVE.
PRE/BETWEEN use target/series event authority; never fabricate a live generation.

Match candidate private binding to active authority BEFORE generation and again
after relevant awaits/before issuance. A fresh stable stamp does not validate an
already stale candidate. A later irrelevant corpus append may reuse a view only
through an explicit verified effective-view equivalence result. Preserve original
generation provenance; factsSha alone does not waive other view/identity changes.
Committed stale records may survive as forensic artifacts with current=false.

A2. FIVE-MINUTE RETENTION AND CLASSIFICATION
Keep qualification180s separate from eligible-state retention300s and from actual
event-to-app latency. A fresh HTTP receipt never proves a fresh game state.
Maintain admittedSourceHead, per-map classifiedSourceHead and lastSuccessfulState.
Every valid committed outcome is classified exactly once; its classification may
be a gap, repeated state, changed state, terminal or integrity rejection.
Only a successfully classified ordinary gap/repeat delegates to the original
retention lease. It does not renew lastConfirmedAt, create a new forecast or imply
an actual pause. Confirmed pauses follow the existing evidence-based policy.
Unclassified newer work fences issuance. An older operation may acknowledge only
its own head, never a later queued head. Identity/terminal/STOP/integrity barriers
remain hard. On restart derive authority from retained proof, not receipt age.

A3. PRIVATE PROJECTED-ROW AUTHORITY
At verified assembly, mint one nonforgeable/private projected-row capability.
Its binding includes the actual source/history context, map/series/side identity,
decision horizon, component row identities, stable function contract, ordered
projection map, values/masks digest and the explicitly authorized target routes.
Journal append and model input delivery require this relation, not independently
borrowed schema/vector objects. Borrowing remains a memory-lifetime primitive.
Same-row reuse across map winner/kills/duration is permitted through explicit
authorized projections. Cross-context pairing fails even if all names and values
coincidentally match. The final owning release or invalid mutation invalidates
the in-memory capability; releasing one borrower does not invalidate other valid
owned borrows. Source freshness expiry forbids CURRENT issuance, not explicitly
forensic/historical replay of an intact authenticated row. Keep integrity lifetime,
borrow lifetime and current eligibility as separate checks.
Persisted JSON cannot mint authority. A source-bound rehydrator must verify pinned
dependencies, semantics and row hashes, then mint an equivalent private capability.
Compiler-only diagnostic fixtures keep a named forensic profile; do not silently
promote them to real source admission. Keep the input-only recorder explicit.
