Architecture and scope
coherentEconomyContextFeatures.mjs is a pure helper called by the existing
observedDraftFeatureBundle projectInteractions before semantic schema hashing.
It reads one coherent admitted observation, the private producer's frozen history
context/relational context and that observation's existing native bundle. It adds
no admission authority, provider access, current-map label, temporal state or
parallel train-only formula. Existing feature columns remain. The component's
definition/schema hash changes with the added versioned definitions; old weights
cannot be assumed compatible. Full family implementation is not claimed.

Inputs are {observation,historyContext,relationalContext,nativeBundle,decisionAt}.
The ten heroes, sides and team identities must form a coherent full draft. Public
accounts may be null. Native economy fields remain literal NW, GPM and XPM with
separate units/missingness. GPM/XPM concentration describes native rate distribution,
not spendable gold. No NW/minute substitution or manual fighting-strength score.

Current formulas and fixed width
38,790 addon columns are explicitly defined, plus the existing separate missing
mask for every value. They contribute to L05, L14 and R06 partial diagnostic scope:
270 team concentration/minute/frozen-context columns; 900 economic-rank columns;
31,500 hero/opposing-distribution/minute/frozen-form columns; 1,620 qualified
objective/team-concentration/closing-style columns; 4,500 qualified objective/
current-hero-NW/closing-style columns. Count is schema width, not useful signal,
data availability, model quality or independent sample size.

For each same-frame five-player nonnegative metric x, S=sum(x), p_i=x_i/S:
top1_share=max(x)/S; top2_share=(largest+second_largest)/S; HHI=sum(p_i^2);
Shannon entropy=-sum(p_i*ln(p_i)) with 0*ln(0)=0; Gini=sum_i sum_j|x_i-x_j|/(10*S);
range=max(x)-min(x). Shares/entropy/Gini need S>0; an observed all-zero range is
zero while its share-based statistics are missing. An absent/invalid operand is
never silently filled with zero. Radiant, Dire and explicit Radiant-Dire products
are retained, with minute and qualified team form/aggression/late-style variants.

Descending economic ranks 1..5 use current literal NW/GPM/XPM, ties by hero ID.
Native value, team share, opposing same-rank difference and positive-denominator
ratio have minute and qualified frozen form variants. Economic rank never means
carry/mid/offlane/support or a farm-role label. Existing native top-share/HHI and
NW/GPM-rank arithmetic has explicit legacy-counterpart metadata where applicable;
different identity/admission boundaries prevent unconditional alias claims.

For every dictionary hero and metric, compare its observed current value v with
the five actual opposing draft heroes: v-opponent_mean, v-opponent_max,
v/opponent_mean, opposing midrank=(count(x<v)+0.5*count(x=v))/5,
(v-opponent_mean)/opponent_population_sd, and v/own_sum-opponent_top1_share.
Undefined ratios/scales are missing. The own-team sum is required only for its
share comparison; a missing teammate metric does not erase an otherwise observed
hero/opponent comparison. Apply side sign, minute, qualified own recent/player-hero/
team/hero-late form and opposing-five-hero late-form mean as separate interactions.
No measured or handcrafted hero fighting strength is asserted. A known absent
hero in the complete observed ten-hero roster has structural zero; this is an
explicit applicability convention, not imputation of a missing active operand.

Historical and objective causality
History comes from the existing private frozen context at original draft cutoff.
Identity validFrom must precede the source receipt; materialization must precede
decisionAt. Per-stat support/minimum/cohort gates apply; a stated availability
timestamp after the cutoff is rejected. Relational closing profiles must bind
the original history content hash/cutoff and be available by decisionAt. Unknown
accounts suppress personal history, preserving qualified hero/team context.

Objective operands are only existing qualified Roshan/Aegis native outputs:
confirmed-death elapsed time, qualified respawn bounds/countdown, qualified alive/
dead flags, confirmed-pickup elapsed/lifetime upper bound and linked confirmed
steal. Native bundle map/observation/raw/profile/receipt must exactly match the
current observation. Raw timer zero/positive is never a new proof of death/alive,
pickup or countdown. Products with team concentration, current hero NW and frozen
closing style are descriptive covariates. They do not attribute Aegis pickup,
ownership or advantage to a side/hero without the existing evidence producer.

Definition pooling
The rich producer keeps at most two immutable definition semantics in a private
LRU pool. Its key binds module/addon versions, source/admission profile, patch and
reviewed rules pin, native contracts, historical producer/support/minute policies,
relational schema/formula version and exact consumed definition/unit IDs, including
definition presence/absence. Current values, history values/content hashes, clocks
and materialization times are not cache keys or cached feature data. The helper's
formula-only schema is one immutable entry for its fixed algebra version.

Cold compilation constructs/freezes names, descriptors and semantic hash once.
Lazy descriptor factories avoid large formula/descriptor construction on warm
frames. Every warm frame checks name/order/count, recomputes values and masks,
and reuses immutable name/definition arrays and hash. Actual semantic changes
select another entry; a third rich semantics evicts the oldest. Callers retaining
returned snapshots hold their own references; the entry bound is not a process
peak-heap guarantee. Diagnostic pool status has no admission authority.
