08. The 720 exact trajectory/history bindings
Name: draft_live.trajectory.{q}.past_{w}s.{stat}.{s}.{key}.product
Value: trajectory(q,w,stat) * H.team(s,currentTeamId).statistics[key].value
The three exact keys and formulas are:
formWinFraction30d: count(selected-team wins)/n, endedAt window30d;
late40Fraction90d: count(durationSeconds>=2400)/n, endedAt window90d;

All history dependencies must have been locally available by t0. Product units
are trajectory-unit * fraction or * seconds. Histories on both sides are separate
features; there is no implied team-specific future comeback probability.

and v3/winMeanDurationSeconds. Operand address MUST also include component,

alone cannot identify a single reference. Two formerly ambiguous keys generate
2*5*4*9*2=720 per LIVE head; the third is already nonambiguous numerically.
