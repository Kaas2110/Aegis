Aegis Astra R7: Steam economy source-document research
Date: 2026-10-26. Read-only public documentation/source inspection. No Steam,
OpenDota or STRATZ provider requests, credentials, training or app changes.

Conclusion
The requested sources support keeping wallet gold, cumulative earned gold and
net worth distinct. They do NOT establish a current Valve implementation formula
mapping GetLiveLeagueGames.gold to net worth, or prove that GetRealtimeStats.net_gold
equals either cumulative earned gold or net worth. Do not join those raw keys just
because their names look similar. Endpoint-specific mapping remains necessary.

3. RDota2
The requested get_match_details documentation was built on 2019-05-01. It defines
FINAL gold as gold left when the game ends, separately from gold_spent. Its live
method page only describes scoreboard as a nested information list; no gold
mapping is supplied. These are third-party R-client docs, not a current Valve
contract. The linked Team Fortress community-wiki live page returned HTTP403 to
the documentation fetch; no bypass or inference from that failure was attempted.

4. Maintained OpenDota source: stronger implementation evidence, distinct scopes
OpenDota is community maintained. Its source is primary evidence of OpenDota's own
implementation, not Valve's private WebAPI server implementation.

Recommended Sol documentation action
Record source endpoint/message, literal field name, interpretation and evidence
separately. Keep GetLiveLeagueGames.net_worth and replay networth_t as explicit
source-qualified net-worth candidates; preserve raw realtime net_gold separately
until its meaning is established. Retain unknown rather than silently equating
wallet, earned and wealth. Research narrowed the uncertainty; it did not justify
discarding a source or declaring all cross-source model features equivalent.
