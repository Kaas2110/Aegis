AEGIS — OPENDOTA SOURCE STUDY AND HISTORICAL/LIVE BRIDGE
26 October 2026. Astra architecture and source review; no application changes.

DECISION
Keep OpenDota for completed-match acquisition and replay-derived historical data.
Keep the new current dynamic decision path on a coherent qualified Steam profile,
with historical player/hero/team context supplied from the local causal store.
OpenDota history CAN support live-model training after causal-prefix and measurement
compatibility checks. It does not have to have been downloaded during the original
game. Such reconstruction is not proof of original live availability or latency.
Neither wholesale replacement of OpenDota by Steam WebAPI nor a blind field-name
substitution is supported. No new provider, replay parser or model was activated.

1. WHAT OPENDOTA ADDS BEYOND A SINGLE STEAM WEBAPI RESPONSE
Core combines several acquisition stages: basic API results, Game Coordinator
metadata, replay parsing, archive data, derived metrics and current enrichment.
The Game Coordinator retriever uses a logged-in Steam client; a WebAPI key alone
does not instantiate that transport. Rich replay data is reconstructed after the
game. Reproducing this entire infrastructure locally is a separate undertaking,
not a consequence of possessing a Steam key.

The public match object merges archive < parsed < GC < API, joining players by
player_slot. has_parsed and other flags mean that a stage blob exists, not that
every desired field is present, temporally qualified or originally observable.
Later refreshes can overwrite blobs. Aegis must keep its own observed versions,
receipt times, identity corrections and immutable dependency hashes.

2. ECONOMY: RESOLVED MEANINGS, NOT GENERIC ALIASES
Canonical net worth is CURRENT inventory value plus wallet, can decrease, and is
the primary current economic measure. This agrees with the owner's clarification.
Cumulative earnings, current assets, current wallet and income rate are distinct.

In the inspected parser:
  m_iNetWorth -> entry.networth -> networth_t.
  m_iTotalEarnedGold -> entry.gold -> gold_t.
The minute team-advantage routine sums signed entry.gold, so radiant_gold_adv in
THIS implementation is earned-gold advantage, not net-worth advantage. Root also
independently read these exact two source regions. Do not retroactively redefine
all old OpenDota rows without establishing their lineage. Preserve old values and
weight contracts; source adapters need explicit semantic versions.

Core total_gold is floor(final gold_per_min * final duration / 60), an earnings
proxy affected by rounding. It cannot recover early GPM or current net worth.
netWorth / elapsedMinutes is not GPM. This source mapping does not determine the
meaning of another Steam endpoint's gold or net_gold merely from its name.

3. FIELD-TO-FEATURE DECISION TABLE
The current map being forecast is distinct from already completed prior maps.
Prior-map final summaries may become legitimate historical form once available.

Field/group | Source meaning | Decision for Aegis
times | relative rounded game seconds at retained minute boundaries | validate
  actual coordinates; latest sample <= cutoff; no future interpolation.
gold_t | cumulative earned gold | earnings trajectory; never native NW alias.
networth_t | optional native m_iNetWorth samples | promising historical counterpart
  to qualified Steam NW; require real presence, alignment and profile comparison.
xp_t | cumulative earned XP | XP trajectory; not automatic XPM/level replacement.
lh_t/dn_t | cumulative last hits/denies | prefix farming/pressure trajectories.
radiant_gold_adv | signed earned-gold sum in inspected parser | qualified earnings
  lead only; team NW needs ten same-time native NW measurements and valid sides.
final GPM/XPM/total_gold | final-map income summaries | prior-map history or labels;
  never substitute for current-map early income.
level/K/D/A | raw interval counters, not complete public per-minute arrays here |
  use retained timed raw/Steam states; final scalars cannot supply early levels.
hero_damage_t/tower_damage_t/camps_stacked_t | optional native minute counters |
  historical candidate; prove alignment and Steam measurement counterpart.
hero_healing_t | derived prefix healing with exclusions | keep exact rules;
  not an automatic alias of a differently defined provider healing counter.
purchase_log | purchase events, recipes excluded; limited initial synthesis |
  purchase milestones, not complete current inventory/ownership/power.
hero_inventory | transient limited-slot collection, omitted from final blob |
  no claim that the API exposes a full historical inventory timeline.
buyback_log | occurred buyback event | age/count after event; not current
  affordability, availability or exact cooldown without qualified rules/state.
objectives | building deaths, Roshan kills and Aegis events | event-qualified
  objective history; not arbitrary current HP or complete initial state.
lane_pos/lane_role/position_est | aggregated lane behavior and inferred role |
  final early-window inference must not appear before its full dependency horizon.
obs/sen logs | placement/removal events | historical vision only with complete
  event/identity rules and a real live counterpart; heatmaps are not current vision.
ability_uses/item_uses | full-map aggregate uses | previous-map style; not present
  ultimate cooldown, mana, readiness or item ownership.
deaths_log/teamfights/pauses | some fields finalized using later events | individual
  causal-horizon rules or separate online prefix accumulator, not timestamp-only.
rank_tier/computed_mmr/benchmarks | request-time current enrichment in core |
  cannot silently describe what was known when an old map occurred.
patch/hero/item dictionaries | calendar inference and versioned game metadata |
  use matching rules/units; current dictionary is not historical truth for all maps.

4. A MATERIAL ALIGNMENT TRAP
times/gold_t/xp_t/lh_t/dn_t append together. Optional networth_t and some other
arrays append only when a property is non-null. A partially absent optional field
can therefore produce a shorter array without its own timestamps. Blindly pairing
its first value with times[0] invents chronology. Reject ambiguous alignment or
use an explicitly timed retained source; do not pad, forward-infer or fill with
final values. Equal lengths alone do not prove every other identity/clock rule.
Seconds-level interval objects are consumed internally and discarded from the
public blob. Parser access to a field is not proof the API preserves its timeline.
https://github.com/odota/parser/blob/927adf6bbd1fd321b711f544a5d754bfe75d89a7/src/main/java/opendota/CreateParsedDataBlob.java#L1387-L1497

5. SPECIFIC FUTURE-INFORMATION COUNTEREXAMPLES
- Death duration is attached to death time but requires a later respawn. Some
  death/gold/reincarnation classifications use later seconds as well.
- A finalized teamfight includes its eventual end, damage/XP outcome and grouping
  based on a gap after the last kill. At an earlier frame that result is unknown.
- Completed pause duration refers to pause start but is emitted at unpause.
- position_est uses farm at minutes10-12 and lane data; it is not a draft/5-minute
  position label. lane_role and hero Carry tags are not player positions1-5.
- Current profile rank/MMR and request-time benchmark percentiles may reflect
  later games. A historical response is not necessarily a historical snapshot.
- Comeback/throw/stomp/loss use the full advantage curve and final winner; current
  online momentum requires its own prefix-only formula.

These are upstream source risks, not a claim that Aegis currently uses every such
field incorrectly. Technical regressions must append later events and verify
already-admissible earlier feature rows remain unchanged; explicit later revisions
remain separate rather than backdating correction knowledge.

6. ROSHAN AND AEGIS: USEFUL POSITIVE EVIDENCE
The parser records Roshan kill time/team and separate Aegis pickup, explicit theft
and denial with recipient slot. Keep these as different events: the Roshan-killing
team need not possess Aegis, and pickup can happen later. Build last-confirmed-death
age, kill count/team, pickup age/recipient and explicit theft/denial evidence.
Where an explicit theft event exists, use its source-qualified meaning; do not
require an invented reconstructed killer identity. Where theft is inferred from
kill/pickup sides, preserve that different derivation and its prerequisites.

No exact next-spawn timer is emitted by this inspected Java blob path. A confirmed
death plus qualified patch rules supports a respawn interval, not an observed exact
second. Alive status can remain unknown inside/after that window without observation.
Steam timer semantics still require independent qualification. Aegis item rules
do not prove possession or pickup time. These features can interact with prior-map
team closing pace/late tendency, hero stage and live advantage; they do not establish
that the earlier ~5.7-minute total-duration error was caused by Roshan.
The owner explicitly extends this context to winners, not just totals. Current
qualified Aegis state belongs to map-win and whole-series LIVE heads as well as
kills/duration. PRE/POST/BETWEEN can use prior-map control/conversion history;
they cannot know future current-map possession. Context includes holder identity,
hero/stage, pickup age, qualified remaining life and coherent economic/draft state.
Do not impose a constant probability bonus: conversion into pressure/objectives
depends on the situation, and possession may be wasted. Historical conversion
statistics describe completed prior episodes under explicit windows/support;
they are not proof of causation and do not admit future current-episode outcomes.

7. DICTIONARIES AND DOCUMENTATION NEED VERSIONED SEMANTICS TOO
Pinned dotaconstants patch.json contains61 base patches, ending7.41, without letter
revisions. This is not current Valve patch verification. Keep Aegis's separate
letter-aware observed/inferred/unknown patch contract and boundary uncertainty.
Today's hero attributes, item costs and mechanics cannot be applied to all old maps.

Two concrete naming/description mismatches warrant regression coverage:
- dotaconstants base_attack_time is assigned BaseAttackSpeed, whereas attack_rate
  is assigned AttackRate. Do not assume base_attack_time is seconds by its name.
- core purchase_time description says last purchase; compute.ts sums purchase
  times per item. first_purchase_time is a separate statistic.
The old fixed lane-efficiency denominator4948 is not a modern hero/patch-specific
expected-farm model. Build a separately named historical empirical reference.

8. PRACTICAL MODEL FEATURES THIS ENABLES
Preserve the complete agreed feature inventory; no importance pruning now.
Qualified historical trajectories support hero x minute economic expectation,
current native NW residual/z-score with support, player-hero form and opponent
comparisons, team economy concentration and stage-specific closing behavior.
Prefix trajectory shape can distinguish income acceleration from current asset
advantage; these are useful separate factors rather than one fabricated strength.
Prior-map Roshan-to-end timing, recovery after map loss and hero/style-specific
late-game behavior may inform LIVE and series heads when historical facts are causal.
Whether these improve prediction is a later experiment, not a result of this study.

9. BOUNDED SOL IMPLEMENTATION AND ACCEPTANCE (R8.6A)
1. Inventory ALREADY retained data by method/stage/build-known-or-unknown, fields,
   time axis, identities, corrections and original availability evidence. No refresh.
2. Create a per-port ledger: raw path -> physical measure -> clock/horizon ->
   canonical feature -> vector index/mask -> restored row -> model input contract.
3. Separate earned gold, wallet, native NW and opaque fields. Trace actual old
   gold-lead contracts before any source migration; never reuse weights silently.
4. Validate array alignment and full causal dependency horizons. Add the specific
   counterexamples above; keep original observation and retrospective reconstruction
   as separate axes from originally issued and replayed features.
5. Compare retained same-map/player/time Steam and OpenDota observations where
   available, with bounded declared skew and no future interpolation. Correlation
   is not equivalence. Report signed differences, missingness and event strata.
6. Assign exact equivalent / explicit causal conversion / distinct quantity /
   historical-only / unavailable to each port. Source coverage denominators must
   include failed eligible polls; paired successful replay samples do not prove95%.
7. Trace one qualified real example end to end where evidence permits, plus negative
   earned-gold-as-NW and future-dependent event cases. Missing paired corpus is a
   specific evidence gate, not permission for guesses or new provider acquisition.
8. Return this mapping with all13 routing/semantics receipts before training.
