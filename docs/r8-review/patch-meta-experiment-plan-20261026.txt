AEGIS — PATCH/META HYPOTHESES AT THE LATER FEATURE-SELECTION STAGE
26 October 2026. Owner clarification supersedes mandatory patch-decay wording.

PLACEMENT AND CURRENT SCOPE
Sequence: complete feature/data contracts -> review/authorization -> fit and
evaluate full eligible boosting baselines -> feature selection/consolidation and
patch/meta experiments -> qualified package review. No experiments or fits now.
The new patch/meta comparisons belong to P6 of the R8 future model plan, AFTER the
full-feature baseline. Do not introduce an automatic age/patch discount or new
adaptive updater into application behavior based on this architectural hypothesis.

Prepare now: exact patch revision, game/source-known times, pro/public population,
contemporaneous high-rank qualification, account/hero/role evidence, causal raw
window counts, completeness and source compatibility. Data provenance and future
exclusion are correctness requirements, not optional ablations. Whether a valid
patch feature, a public-match reference or an old-data weighting policy improves
prediction is an empirical question deferred to the selection stage.

HYPOTHESES, NOT CONCLUSIONS
H1. Recent same-letter-patch hero statistics improve some professional forecasts.
H2. High-ranked public games reveal useful hero/meta/player information sooner
    than the smaller professional population, including verified pro accounts.
H3. Older patches may retain substantial information about player/team skill,
    decision-making and some hero interactions despite balance changes.
H4. Relevance may differ by hero, changed mechanic, model phase and target; one
    global age discount may be inferior to no discount or contextual treatment.
H5. Fast feature-statistic updates and slower boosting refits may help new patches,
    but adaptation can also overfit small samples or react to noise.
None is a measured Aegis result. Do not assume shared players remove the pub/pro
domain difference; no claim that every high-ranked player is professional.

CONTROLLED COMPARISONS AFTER FULL BASELINES
Use one pinned dataset calendar, global chronological series split and common
eligible evaluation maps/minutes for each comparison. Preserve original/rebuilt
evidence labels, source masks and all unrelated feature/model settings.
1. Patch/meta feature-family ablations and semantic consolidation against the
   full-feature baseline. Record useful subgroup effects, not only overall mean.
2. Uniform treatment of eligible history versus age-based weighting versus
   patch-aware weighting. No decay is the legitimate control, not a failed default.
   Owner explicitly raised sample age weighting as an OPTIONAL experiment, not an
   implementation request. A possible candidate is exponential decay
   w_age = exp(-ln(2) * age_days / half_life_days), with half-life selected only on
   earlier validation folds; no half-life is chosen now. Compute age relative to
   the relevant training decision cutoff, not today's date for every old fold.
   Multiply the existing map/series balancing weights and distribute frame weights
   within that unit; repeated live frames must not acquire independent map weight.
   Test age and patch effects separately before a justified interaction. Report
   effective sample support, rare-hero loss and recent-versus-older cohort effects.
   Keep the uniform-age-weight control and identical held-out maps/minutes.
3. Long history versus recent windows versus current-revision-only history where
   enough data exists. Compare on common test rows and disclose differing support.
4. Professional-only meta, separately qualified high-rank public meta, verified
   professional-player public history and their combinations. Outcome training
   targets remain professional by default; direct pub-to-pro target transfer is a
   separately declared experiment, never silent pooling.
5. Alternative causal window lengths;7/14/30 days are candidate configurations,
   not selected optimal constants. Test support/uncertainty signals as features.
6. If earlier results justify it, compare fixed pooled boosting, statistic-only
   refresh, controlled refit and a regularized adaptation candidate. Small-sample
   smoothing/relevance parameters are learned on earlier TRAIN folds only.
   No implicit online learning or automatic model activation follows.

Do not run the full combinatorial cross-product. Predeclare bounded main effects
and a small justified interaction set; extend only on validation evidence and
within an explicitly authorized compute budget. Keep full-set artifacts intact.

PATCH TRANSITION VALIDATION
Replay several historical patch transitions in chronological order. At every
decision use only data and labels actually permitted before that cutoff under the
declared original/reconstructed profile. Never calculate a patch's full-month
win rate and feed it into its first day's examples. Parameter/feature selection
uses earlier development transitions; final locked transitions stay untouched.
Report first-day/early/supported phases by actual elapsed time AND available
sample counts. A fixed number of elapsed days does not guarantee adequate data.

Evaluate map winner, series winner and PRE/POST/LIVE kills/duration separately.
For LIVE use fixed same-map landmarks and one80+ group; repeated frames are not
independent examples. Report equal-strength/strong-team/comeback and sparse hero/
role cohorts, sample support and appropriate map/series clustered uncertainty.
Metrics: winner logloss/Brier/calibration/accuracy; totals MAE plus distribution
scores and interval coverage/width; availability, latency and memory as constraints.
An apparent aggregate gain cannot excuse a causal violation or widespread loss
on the agreed priority cohorts. A tiny subgroup gain is not proof without enough
independent support. Holdout is never reused to tune weights/thresholds.

SELECTION OUTCOMES
Accept a patch/meta policy only if the declared validation criteria support it.
Otherwise retain the simpler existing policy and record the result. A feature
family can help early-patch or equal-strength cases without improving the global
average; preserve such effects only with reproducible subgroup evidence. No fixed
number of features, compulsory discount or promised accuracy is prescribed.
After an accepted reduction, compile dependency masks so unused branches are not
computed. Keep full feature catalogue and reversible package/version lineage.

adaptation strategies, including retaining/forgetting historical information.
It does not establish which policy works for Aegis or which Dota patch matters.
