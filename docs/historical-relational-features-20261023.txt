AEGIS HISTORICAL RELATIONAL FEATURE COMPONENT
23 October 2026. Diagnostic implementation; no model fit or serving admission.

Purpose

historicalRelationalFeatures.mjs implements deterministic prior-map statistics
for the shared feature producer. It preserves separate information about each
player, hero, team and conditional combination. It does not invent a single
weighted "true strength" score or infer farm roles from roster order.

Inputs and phases

produceHistoricalRelationalFeatures receives a validated observed-draft history
context and the exact pinned prior-map fact bytes to which that context is bound.
The original first-complete-draft history cutoff remains fixed. A later account
disclosure does not move the cutoff. Context validation establishes semantic
integrity, not independent receipt authentication; actual raw replay belongs
upstream. The dynamic bridge retains its own opaque proof of that replay.

producePrematchHistoricalRelationalFeatures uses a separate pinned
aegis.prematch-history-target.v1 input: matchId, knownAt, historyCutoffAt, explicit
team sides and optional known accounts. Current heroId must be null. This is a
host-declared PRE identity receipt, explicitly not independently authenticated.
No fake observed draft is created. Selected-hero columns remain null/masked and
carry current_draft scope so the PRE projection can exclude them. Known accounts
are sorted for deterministic slots; up to five unknown placeholders per side
are allowed. No slot is a role or strength rank.

Both routes exclude the current map, require prior outcomes and every used
roster/performance fact to be available by the declared cutoff, distinguish
exact nanosecond boundaries, and require a complete bounded selector scope.
Unknown accounts do not erase available team/hero history. Hero populations use
known heroes across the selected local parsed professional population, including
anonymous players. This does not claim full population coverage, role adjustment,
Immortal/pub coverage or a causal effect of a pick.

Implemented deterministic scope

There are 319 logical selectors with 7-, 30- and 90-day windows: team and direct
team opponent; player; player-hero; player-team; player-team-hero; hero; team-hero;
ally player pair; player-on-hero with ally player; player-on-hero with ally hero;
exact two-player/two-hero pair; hero pair; hero triple; exact three-player/hero
combination; cross-side hero, player and player-on-hero counters.

Each selector has distinct-map support, recency-weight effective count, raw and
fixed Beta(10,10) smoothed win fractions, exponentially decayed fractions,
binomial uncertainty, previous-map duration and total-kill distributions,
late-game fractions, win/loss-conditioned duration, team kill pace and recency.
Smoothing is deterministic; it is not learned target encoding. Statistics below
minimum support remain missing. Zero observed support does not prove that the
entity never played: the population is the explicitly available parsed cohort.

The optional heroPerformances namespace uses the exact
aegis.prior-player-native-performance.v1 field contract and native source mapping.
It supplies previous-map GPM, XPM, KDA components, last hits, denies, damage,
healing, level, final net worth, total gold/XP, wards and gold spent when retained.
Per-minute values and efficiency ratios have explicit formulas. Native GPM,
final net worth and total gold remain distinct. Missing gold_spent stays missing.
The 32 native/derived metrics produce prior player, player-on-hero, team and hero
profiles, player-on-hero minus self, player-on-hero minus hero population, and a
population sample-SD normalization. The team scalar mean requires all five heroes
to have that metric; a four-player average is not relabelled as a complete team.

The fixed v1 schema has 49,388 ordered values with separate null masks. Profiles
expose 208 player, 105 team and 38 hero keys with support and units. No feature
was selected by predictive correlation. This new component does not claim to be
an exact numerical port of all older Python research vectors. Their pinned names
and the full 79-family catalogue remain preserved in the registry; unsupported
and partially implemented families retain their explicit status.

Output and practical use

Every ordered descriptor records family, formula version, units, input scope,
window, support reference and latest dependency availability. The semantic schema
hash includes phase, formulas, fixed smoothing, support policy and native field
mapping. Exact supporting map IDs/times are retained once in a deduplicated
supportRows pool. Receipt authenticity must not be inferred from a hash alone.

Compute this component once per immutable history/identity revision, not on each
live poll or card view. Reuse compact values, masks and schema/context references.
The fully described diagnostic object is large and is not a proposed mobile API
response. A synthetic 1,000-map scaling probe is development evidence only; it
does not establish a hard latency/memory bound for the maximum input. A production
cache builder still needs appropriate bounded execution and failure handling.

Open scope

Independent personal MMR, learned opponent/role adjustment, patch-letter meta,
Immortal population, ban history, hero mechanics, item/ward timing, buyback events,
and finishing speed conditioned on an earlier lead require additional supported
inputs or future TRAIN-bound state. Win-conditioned previous duration is a useful
descriptive proxy, not evidence that a team closes a known gold lead quickly.
No current-map final scalar, current-map outcome or future-map draft is admitted.

Technical synthetic checks cover exact formulas; map and player permutation;
current/future and duplicate exclusion; +1 ns gates; anonymous populations;
conditional pair/triple/counter identity; metric units, known zero and missing;
partial/complete cohort rules; PRE phase boundaries; content/schema integrity;
and provenance deduplication. No provider call, model training, HPO, calibration,
quality evaluation or public promotion was performed for this component.
