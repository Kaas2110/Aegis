Purpose and result
The prior implementation could retain original responses larger than the old
facts envelope but did not supply a bounded normalized map view to the real
history formulas. Its compact persistence was separately tested while the actual
bootstrap/consumer still used the expanded v1 path. This block adds an explicit,
disabled diagnostic route across those seams and bounds exhaustive schema audit.
This is a source-only diagnostic block, not completion of R6 or a training gate.
