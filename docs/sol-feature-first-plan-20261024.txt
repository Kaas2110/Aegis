ARCHITECTURE TO PRESERVE

1. One logical pure feature producer; modules behind it are appropriate. Offline
   replay and the background runtime call that producer with identical pinned
   source inputs, schema, rules and historical context. No second train-only formula.
2. One current-state source profile per frame. Do not combine a newer RT economy
   packet with an older GLG score as if simultaneous. A profile switch is explicit
   and resets incompatible temporal windows; it does not erase map phase/clock
   continuity. Retained historical OpenDota facts may join Steam live observations.
   They have a separate immutable cutoff and never become current measurements.
3. At first observed full draft t0, freeze prior-map history at t0. Account/team
   disclosure at t1 creates a new identity/context revision valid at t1; its history
   cutoff remains t0. Preserve old outputs. Unknown accounts leave individual
   selectors missing but do not erase valid team/hero history.
4. Current-map results/final metrics are labels, never early predictors. Results
   of previous maps in the series become inputs only after their actual receipt.
   Historical outcomes may determine historical winrates only within their cutoff.
5. Keep game clock, request/receipt time, source availability, historical cutoff,
   identity validFrom, materialization time, decision cutoff, calculation start/end
   and trace commit time separate. A hash proves byte identity, not provider truth.
6. Main card: winner of the entire BO1/BO3/BO5 series. Detail: selected map's winner
   and kill/time totals. Manual past-map selection retains its own heroes/results.
   Do not redesign the interface. New candidates remain diagnostic until reviewed.
