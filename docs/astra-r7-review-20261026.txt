F1 / P1 — CLASSIFIED SOURCE GAP IS STILL TREATED AS PENDING
retainedFeaturePipeline.mjs:89-104,208,218-234.
An admitted HTTP500/empty-list/missing-map receipt is processed, but does not
advance the map's processedSourceKey. The reader rejects with
newer_committed_source_pending before consulting the owned five-minute retention
state. Thus one ordinary gap immediately hides a previously eligible forecast.
Separate admitted head, fully classified head and last successful observation.
A genuinely pending head still fences issuance; a fully classified ordinary gap
preserves only the original eligible trace/deadline. No receipt-based renewal.
Terminal, identity conflict, STOP and failed proof remain hard barriers.

F2 / P1 — STATIC CURRENT AUTHORITY IS NOT BOUND TO THE ACTUAL PROOF
staticDiagnosticWriter.mjs:17-25,35-44,60-63.
Before/after stamps can be equal while both refer to a newer context than the
already stale candidate proof for the same map/series. Equality during IO alone
does not prove the candidate was current on entry. Bind expected authority to the
private proof, compare at entry and after IO. Keep frozen historical binding,
active target/series binding and current live source fence distinct. A live poll
generation is not a historical corpus generation or facts hash.

F3 / P1 — TWO GENUINE BUT UNRELATED SCHEMA/VECTOR OBJECTS CAN BE PAIRED
featureProjectionCompiler.mjs:13-22,117-132;
diagnosticModelInputDelivery.mjs:17-28,45-58.
The private vector authenticates names/count, separately from the schema's exact
authority. A.schema plus B.vector with identical names/count can produce a

Mint a private projection/row capability at verified assembly; bind context,
semantics, values/masks and authorized head projections. Preserve legitimate
same-row multihead reuse. A stable model contract alone cannot identify a row.
