AEGIS — ASTRA FEATURE SEMANTICS AND TARGET CORRECTIONS
24 October 2026. Diagnostic source corrections; no training or serving admission.

Findings and decisions

1. Series models consume a team1-oriented target, while current-map history/live
   features are Radiant/Dire-oriented. The old component omitted their explicit
   bridge. produceSeriesContext now accepts optional currentMapSides containing
   mapId, seriesId, radiantTeamId, direTeamId and availableAt. It validates exact
   known teams, current-map identity and chronology. Two additive features encode
   team1/team2 side as +1 Radiant or -1 Dire. Unknown sides remain missing. They
   are eligible only for SERIES_PRE/SERIES_LIVE; no next-map side is guessed
   between maps. Root supplies this binding from validated identity, never IDs'
   numerical order. The shared series schema has 44 columns, series-causal.v2.

2. Broad family target lists excluded safe historical subsets from undrafted
   targets: teammate history P01, player-team H10, past performance ratios H11,
   player aggregates H12, overall player late/duration H14 and player-versus-
   opponent-player history P05. The explicit historical-target-eligibility policy
   expands only these six families' union
   to PRE_MAP_WIN/PRE_KILLS/PRE_DURATION/SERIES_PRE. Individual current_draft,
   current_live and current_map_label scope guards remain mandatory. Selected
   hero history is still prohibited before the draft. This preserves all 79
   families and original catalogue alternatives; it is not feature pruning.

3. Some metric metadata overstated effective support. For example, a kill mean
   based on three complete maps inherited an effective count near five from the
   wider five-map outcome cohort. Win-only duration had the same defect. The
   corrected effectiveCount is (sum(w)^2)/sum(w^2) over that metric's contributing
   map subset, with unchanged explicit decay. Formula version is now
   aegis.prior-map-relational-statistics.v2. Schema hashes change accordingly.

4. Team native performance already averaged all five same-side player values.
   Descriptors now state that explicitly, including that a mean player ratio is
   calculated per player before the five-player mean. These features are not
   team sums or ratios of team totals. This correction changes their schema
   description, not their existing numerical aggregation.

5. The prior-performance mapper now rejects duplicate native player_slot values,
   even if hero/account IDs differ. A malformed five-row side cannot masquerade
   as five independent valid player slots.

6. Current-series winner changes/recovery counts are classified as Q02 observed
   previous-map context. They are not Q03 historical recovery skill. Their
   numerical values are unchanged. Detailed historical recovery remains pending.

Verification and limits

Nine new synthetic semantic regression tests failed against the installed
baseline and passed against the staged corrections. The historical, native
performance and series regression group passed 58 tests; five additional target
policy tests passed. An actual canonical-catalogue/shared-producer check proved
24 safe subset/target links missing before the eligibility correction and present
after it (six features across four undrafted targets). The same projections
contained no current_draft/current_live definitions. These are technical synthetic
controls, not additional real-provider evidence or model quality measurements.

The current POST projection still needs explicit confirmed draft hero identities
and side encodings before future training; selecting historical cohorts does not
replace all hero identity main effects. Full family implementation, verified
roles, patch/letter/population conditioning, causal opponent-style response,
historical series recovery, rich BETWEEN identity, C1/C2/C3 reconciliation and
source coverage/resource gates remain open. No simple or compact model is
silently substituted. Training/HPO/calibration/quality evaluation remain paused.
